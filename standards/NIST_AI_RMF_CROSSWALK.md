## Operational Gap

NIST AI RMF establishes governance expectations but does not operationalize runtime behavioral enforcement for decentralized edge AI systems.

HHI introduces runtime governance infrastructure capable of maintaining:
- telemetry continuity
- escalation visibility
- stop authority persistence
- behavioral drift detection
during active execution.
