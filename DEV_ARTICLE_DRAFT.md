# Local AI Has a Governance Problem Nobody Is Solving

Local AI systems are spreading faster than governance infrastructure.

Phones.
Offline agents.
Raspberry Pis.
Edge devices.
Multimodal local systems.

Everyone talks about:
- performance
- privacy
- ownership
- lower cost

Almost nobody talks about what disappears when AI leaves centralized infrastructure.

The governance layer disappears too.

Cloud systems at least leave behind:
- moderation layers
- telemetry
- logging
- provider oversight
- audit visibility

Local AI removes much of that infrastructure.

Now systems operate directly on-device with little runtime oversight.

That creates a governance problem.

Not theoretical risk.

Operational drift.

Behavior accumulates over time while visibility weakens.

This is where HHI_Local_AI_Governance_Framework started.

The repository introduces:
- Execution-Time Governance
- Behavioral Drift monitoring
- Decision Boundaries
- Stop Authority enforcement
- runtime telemetry
- Continuous Assurance

The objective is not merely to document acceptable behavior.

The objective is to operationalize governance during runtime execution itself.

Time turns behavior into infrastructure.
Behavior is the most honest data there is.
