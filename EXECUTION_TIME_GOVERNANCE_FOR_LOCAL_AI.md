# EXECUTION_TIME_GOVERNANCE_FOR_LOCAL_AI

## What Is Happening

Local and edge AI systems are increasingly operating outside centralized governance environments.

Offline agents, multimodal edge systems, IoT AI deployments, and local reasoning models reduce:
- centralized logging
- moderation visibility
- escalation oversight
- provider accountability
- runtime telemetry continuity

This creates Governance Drift.

## Why It Fails

Most governance frameworks remain document-oriented instead of execution-oriented.

Policies describe expected behavior.
Runtime systems produce actual behavior.

Without runtime governance:
- Behavioral Drift accumulates invisibly
- unsafe interactions normalize
- escalation pathways decay
- accountability diffuses
- oversight becomes post-hoc

## What HHI Introduces

HHI introduces Execution-Time Governance infrastructure through:
- Decision Boundaries
- Stop Authority
- Governance Telemetry
- Interaction Trace continuity
- Continuous Assurance

## Failure Pattern Mapping

| Failure Pattern | Missing Capability | HHI Control | Evidence Produced |
|---|---|---|---|

| Escalation suppression | Human intervention | Stop Authority | Escalation records |
